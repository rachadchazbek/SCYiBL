# Label definitions

SCYiBL assigns each journaling sentence zero or more of these 8 categories (multi-label).

**All definitions are provisional.** They are working hypotheses copied from
`scyibl/taxonomy.py`, which is the single source of truth, until they are checked against the
original annotation guide (#3). The meanings of the acronyms **CP** and **ED** are not known.
Do not present any definition here as confirmed.

This page deliberately contains no dataset examples (privacy rules in `CLAUDE.md`).
`tests/test_data_no_extra.py` checks that it stays in sync with the taxonomy.

## CP

Status: unconfirmed. Identifier: `cp`.

What the acronym stands for is unknown (#3); "cognitive processing" below is a working hypothesis.

Cognitive processing of experience: recalling, narrating or making sense of past events, situations and decisions, and their consequences.

## Constructivism

Status: unconfirmed. Identifier: `constructivism`.

Constructing personal meaning: reinterpreting experiences, building a new narrative or perspective about oneself, others or events.

## Coping

Status: unconfirmed. Identifier: `coping`.

Concrete strategies used to deal with stress or difficulties, such as problem-solving, planning, seeking support or taking a break.

## ED

Status: unconfirmed. Identifier: `ed`.

What the acronym stands for is unknown (#3); the definition below is a working hypothesis.

Working hypothesis, acronym unconfirmed: expressions of relief, acceptance, gratitude or growth in one's outlook on life.

## ER/SR

Status: unconfirmed. Identifier: `er_sr`.

Emotion regulation / self-regulation: noticing, expressing or managing emotions and moods, including negative states such as anxiety, sadness or irritability.

## Empowerment

Status: unconfirmed. Identifier: `empowerment`.

A sense of agency, confidence or control: believing one can change things or wanting to become a stronger, more capable person.

## Exposure/Habit

Status: unconfirmed. Identifier: `exposure_habit`.

Gradual exposure to feared situations, or building repeated habits and routines.

## PERMA

Status: unconfirmed. Identifier: `perma`.

Well-being in Seligman's PERMA model: positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning or accomplishment.
